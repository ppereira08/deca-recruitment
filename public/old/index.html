What is DECA?

Program · 04 Q&A

FAQ

Do I need business experience to join?

Nope! Most members join with zero experience. Practices and workshops teach you everything you need.

How much does membership cost?

About $20 total for the year, which covers national/state dues and competition registration. Transportation and appropriate attire are necessary.

Can I do DECA and still play a sport?

Yes! Most events only require prep on your own schedule plus a few competition days.
