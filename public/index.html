What is DECA?

A career and technical student organization that turns classroom theory into competitive, hands-on business experience.

Compete

Role-play and written-event competitions against students from across the region, state, and nation.

Real Skills

Public speaking, networking, financial literacy, and marketing strategy.

Travel

Qualify for the International Career Development Conference and experience new cities with your chapter.

Community

Leadership opportunities, chapter activities, and service projects that build experience beyond the classroom.

The Season

From the first chapter meeting to the international stage.

  1. Sep–Oct

    Chapter Meetings

    Meeting location and times to be determined. Pick your event, learn the format, and prepare through practice role-plays and written events.

  2. Dec.

    District Competition

    Your first competitive stage. Top performers advance to the state conference.

  3. Feb.

    State Conference

    Competition, networking, leadership opportunities, and the chance to qualify for ICDC.

  4. Apr.

    ICDC

    The International Career Development Conference brings DECA members from around the world together for competition, leadership development, and networking.

FAQ

Do I need business experience to join?

Nope! Most members join with little or no prior business experience. Practices and workshops teach you what you need to compete.

How much does membership cost?

Estimated annual dues are around $20. The exact amount will be confirmed once chapter registration and official state and national costs are finalized. Competition-related travel and appropriate attire may involve additional costs.

Can I do DECA and still play a sport?

Yes. Most preparation can be completed around your existing schedule, with competitions and conferences occurring on specific event days.
